# **Emotio**

A beautifully crafted **iOS mood tracking app** built with **SwiftUI** and **SwiftData**. Emotio lets users **log their moods using expressive emojis**, view their emotional history, and personalize their experience with **themes and accent colors**. Whether you're feeling 🤩 or 🥺, Emotio helps you stay in touch with yourself—one mood at a time.

---

## **🌟 Features**

### **🧠 Mood Logging**  
- Log your mood by **tapping an emoji**.  
- Choose from a curated list of over **90+ emotions**.  
- Each emoji displays a **unique motivational message**.

### **📈 Mood History**  
- See a **chronological list** of your logged moods.  
- Entries include **emoji, date, and time**.  
- Swipe to **delete** individual mood logs.

### **⚙️ Settings Panel**  
- Built with **SwiftUI Forms**.
- Select from **12+ accent colors**.
- Toggle between **Light, Dark, or System mode**.
- View app version and info in an **About** section.
